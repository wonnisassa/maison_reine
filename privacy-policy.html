Privacy and Care

How We Protect & Honor Your Personal Information

“At Maison Reine, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our website or interact with our services.”

— Maison Reine Legal & Data Ethics

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Maison Reine, we may collect certain information to provide an exceptional shopping experience, including:

  • Personal Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you place an order or contact our atelier.
  • Contact Information: Details you provide when communicating with us through WhatsApp, Instagram, or email.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device specifications, browser type, and navigation activity to improve our site performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Process, package, and safely dispatch your orders.
  • Respond promptly to styling consultations and client care inquiries.
  • Provide updates regarding limited capsule drops and private previews.
  • Continuously enhance our digital experience and shopping comfort.
  • Maintain website integrity, fraud prevention, and security.

3. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website utilizes essential cookies and lightweight analytics to understand visitor preferences, remember shopping bags, and preserve your customized session settings.

You are free to disable cookies at any time via your browser preferences without losing fundamental site navigation.

4. Information Sharing & Non-Disclosure

Maison Reine does not sell, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Information is shared only with verified logistics partners (such as couriers) and secure payment processors solely to fulfill your orders and deliver your garments in pristine condition.

5. Data Security

We employ strict security practices to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. All transaction information is protected with industry-standard encryption protocols.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You maintain full control over your personal data. At any time, you may:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold on your account.
  • Request correction or rectification of outdated contact details.
  • Request complete deletion of your records from our mailing systems.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications or private catalogues.

7. Third-Party Platforms

Our site may contain links to external channels, including Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp. Please note that external platforms maintain independent privacy frameworks, and we encourage reviewing their terms when navigating outside our domain.

8. Policy Updates

Maison Reine reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy as our atelier expands. All revisions are immediately reflected on this page with an updated timestamp.
